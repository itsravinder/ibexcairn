**One material hit.** A live US registration for **IBEX** in Classes 9 and 42 whose goods description covers *"downloadable computer software for integrating and automating separate software systems"* — which is, almost word for word, what Ibex Cairn is.

### Why this matters more than the earlier findings

The previously documented collisions sit in adjacent categories: [IBEX Limited](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/ibex/) is customer-experience/BPO, and [Ibexa](https://www.ibexa.co/about-ibexa) is a digital experience platform. A coexistence argument for either is plausible on market-separation grounds.

This registration is different: the goods description **is our product category**, in the classes we would file in. Likelihood-of-confusion analysis is multi-factor and a composite mark carrying a second distinctive element ("Cairn") is not automatically barred — but this is no longer a formality, and it is not a question I can answer.

**"Ibex" is a crowded register.** That cuts both ways: crowding can weaken any single owner's claim to exclusivity, but it also raises the odds of an opposition from someone.

## "Cairn" as the distinguishing element

No software-category conflict surfaced. Existing users are in unrelated fields — Cairn Energy (oil and gas), Cairn Capital (financial services, [marks held](https://trademarks.justia.com/owners/cairn-capital-group-limited-2233691/)), Cairn.info (French academic publishing), Cairn (outdoor subscription boxes), Cairn Kids (clothing).

So the second word is doing real distinguishing work, which is the argument in the composite mark's favour.

## The timing argument

**Renaming costs almost nothing today and a great deal later.** As of now: 6 commits, no domain purchased, no organisation registered, no customers, no marketing, no published product. The repository could be renamed in an afternoon.

That window closes the moment money goes into the brand. So the rational sequence is **legal opinion first, brand investment second** — the reverse of what most projects do, and the reason this screening happened at S00 rather than after a launch.
